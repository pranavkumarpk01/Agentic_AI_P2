---
title: Leave Policy (India)
department: HR
region: India
year: 2025
doc_type: policy
---
Full-time employees in India receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave is credited at the start of each quarter, six days at a time.

Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year. Employees must apply for leave in the HR portal at least one week in advance.
