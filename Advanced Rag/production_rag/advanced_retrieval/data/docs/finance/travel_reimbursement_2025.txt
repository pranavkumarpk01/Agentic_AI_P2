---
title: Travel Reimbursement Policy
department: Finance
region: Global
year: 2025
doc_type: policy
---
Taxi and ride-hailing fares between home and the airport are reimbursed for approved business trips.

To get reimbursed, submit a travel expense claim in the expense portal within 30 days and attach all receipts.

Meal expenses on business trips are reimbursed up to 1,500 INR per day in India and 50 USD per day in the US.
