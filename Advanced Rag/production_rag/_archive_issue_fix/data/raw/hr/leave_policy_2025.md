# Leave Policy (2025 edition)

## Parental leave
Employees are entitled to 18 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption.

## Annual leave
Employees get 24 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year.
