# Leave Policy (2027 edition)

## Parental leave
Employees are entitled to 20 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave must be taken within 18 months of the birth or adoption.

## Annual leave
Employees get 26 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Up to 8 unused days carry over to the next year.
