# Leave Policy (2023 edition)

## Parental leave
Employees get 12 weeks of paid parental leave. The leave must be taken within 6 months of the birth or adoption.

## Annual leave
Employees get 18 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Unused days lapse at the end of the year.
