# Travel Booking Guide

## Booking hotels
Book every hotel through the company travel portal. Hotels booked outside the portal are not reimbursed. Choose a hotel close to the office or the client site.

## Booking flights
Book flights at least 14 days ahead. Economy class is the default for all employees.

## Cancelling a hotel
To cancel a hotel booking, use the travel portal at least 48 hours before check-in.
