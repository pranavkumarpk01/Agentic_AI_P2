# Travel and Expense Policy

## Meals
When travelling for work, employees can claim meals up to Rs 1,200 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Hotels
Hotel stays are capped at Rs 8,000 per night in metro cities and Rs 5,000 per night elsewhere.

## Receipts
A receipt is needed for any expense above Rs 500. Smaller expenses need only a short description.
