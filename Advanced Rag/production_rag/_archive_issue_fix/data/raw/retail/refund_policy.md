# Refund and Returns Policy

## Standard returns
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Refunds are processed within 7 working days. Store credit is offered if the original payment method is no longer available. Gift receipts are accepted. Ask in shop.

## Exceptions for electronics
Electronics have a shorter window. Electronics may be returned within 15 days of delivery, and only if the item is unopened and sealed. Opened electronics can only be exchanged if they are faulty.

## Items that cannot be returned
Perishable goods, personalised items and gift cards cannot be returned or refunded.

Call the store desk.
