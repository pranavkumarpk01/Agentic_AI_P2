# Acme Technologies - HR Policy (SAMPLE - replace with your own HR policy)

## 1. Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday to Friday. Employees may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, when all employees are expected to be available.

## 2. Casual and Sick Leave
Every employee gets 12 casual leaves and 8 sick leaves per calendar year. Casual leaves cannot be carried forward to the next year. Sick leave of more than 2 consecutive days requires a medical certificate.

## 3. Earned Leave
Employees earn 1.5 days of earned leave per month, totalling 18 days per year. Up to 30 days of earned leave can be carried forward. Unused earned leave above this limit lapses on December 31.

## 4. Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is provided as per the Maternity Benefit Act of India. The company provides additional support such as a phased return to work. Paternity leave is 10 working days, which must be taken within three months of the birth of the child.

## 5. Public Holidays
The company follows the holiday list published by the state government. A list of 10 holidays is shared with employees in January each year. Employees may choose 2 optional holidays from a list of 6.

## 6. Provident Fund and Gratuity
The company contributes to the Provident Fund as per the rates set by the EPFO. Gratuity is paid as per the Payment of Gratuity Act to employees who complete five years of continuous service.

## 7. Notice Period
The notice period is 60 days for confirmed employees. Employees on probation have a notice period of 15 days. Early release is at the discretion of the manager and HR.

## 8. Reimbursements
Internet reimbursement of up to 1000 rupees per month is available for employees working from home. Bills must be submitted by the 5th of the following month.

## 9. Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to two days per week with manager approval, as mentioned under working hours. The company provides a laptop and a monitor on request. Employees must connect through the company VPN and must not use public Wi-Fi for company work. Employees living outside their city of employment for more than two weeks need written approval from HR.

## 10. Attendance and Timesheets
Attendance is marked through the office app on the first login of the day. Arriving more than 30 minutes late three times in a month is counted as a half day of casual leave. Timesheets must be submitted every Friday by 5:00 PM. Missing timesheets can delay project billing and may be raised with the manager.

## 11. Health Insurance
Every employee is covered under the group health insurance policy from the first day of joining. The cover is 5 lakh rupees for the employee, spouse and up to two children. Parents can be added by paying an extra premium, which is deducted from salary. Cashless treatment is available at network hospitals through the insurance provider. Claims for non-network hospitals must be submitted within 30 days of discharge.

## 12. Bonus and Increments
The annual performance bonus depends on the employee rating and the company results. Salary increments are reviewed once a year and take effect from April. A joining bonus, if given, must be repaid in full if the employee resigns within 12 months of joining. Employees under a performance improvement plan are not eligible for increments in that cycle.

## 13. Leave Encashment
Earned leave can be encashed only at the time of separation from the company. A maximum of 30 days can be encashed, calculated on the basic salary divided by 30 for each day. Casual leave and sick leave cannot be encashed. Leave encashment is paid along with the full and final settlement.

## 14. Bereavement and Marriage Leave
Employees get 5 days of bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member, which means a parent, spouse, child or sibling. Marriage leave is 5 working days and can be taken only once during employment. Marriage leave must be applied for at least two weeks in advance and the invitation or marriage certificate may be requested.

## 15. Leave Without Pay and Sabbatical
Leave without pay of up to 30 days in a year can be approved by the manager and HR when all other leave is used up. Employees who complete five years of service can apply for an unpaid sabbatical of up to 3 months. The job position is protected during the sabbatical but the salary, insurance and bonus are paused.

## 16. Resignation and Exit Process
An employee who wishes to leave must submit the resignation in writing to the manager and HR. The notice period is explained in the notice period section. During the notice period the employee must complete a knowledge transfer plan, return the laptop and access cards, and attend an exit interview with HR. The last working day is confirmed in writing by HR.

## 17. Full and Final Settlement
The full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day. It includes pending salary, leave encashment and gratuity if eligible, minus any recoveries such as unserved notice period or unreturned equipment. Income tax is deducted from the settlement amount as per the Income Tax Act. A relieving letter and experience letter are issued after the settlement is complete.

## 18. Code of Conduct and POSH
The company has zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination or bribery. Complaints of sexual harassment are handled by the Internal Committee, which is formed as required by the POSH Act. All complaints are kept confidential and no employee is punished for raising a complaint in good faith. All employees must complete the conduct training every year.

## 19. Learning and Development
Every employee gets 30000 rupees per year for courses, books or certifications related to their work. Courses above 20000 rupees need manager approval and a service commitment of 12 months after completion. Employees can also take up to 2 paid study days per year to prepare for an approved certification exam.

## 20. Performance Review
Performance is reviewed twice a year, in April and October. Employees are rated from 1 to 5 by their manager after a self review. A rating of 1 or 2 may lead to a performance improvement plan of 60 days with clear goals. Ratings of 4 and 5 are considered for promotion and a higher bonus.

## 21. Travel Reimbursement
Domestic flights are booked in economy class and trains in the second AC class or equivalent. The hotel limit is 5000 rupees per night in metro cities and 3500 rupees in other cities. Daily food allowance for official travel is 800 rupees. Travel claims with bills must be submitted within 15 days of returning from the trip.

## 22. Payroll and Tax Deduction
Salary is credited on the last working day of each month. Payslips are available on the employee portal. Income tax is deducted every month as per the Income Tax Act and the tax regime chosen by the employee. Investment declarations must be submitted in April, and proofs must be submitted by January to avoid extra deduction.
